PAYMENT LOGOS
=============
Drop your real logo files in this folder with these exact names (png, svg, webp, jpg or jpeg all work):

    gpay.png      -> Google Pay
    phonepe.png   -> PhonePe
    paytm.png     -> Paytm
    upi.png       -> Other UPI app
    cod.png       -> Cash on Delivery (optional)

Restart is not needed: refresh the cart page and the logos appear on the payment tiles
(and next to the payment method on the bill). Until a file is added, a built-in text badge is shown.
Best size: about 300 x 100 px, transparent or white background.
