Linear data structures, step by step

  • Linked list: nodes joined by next pointers. Insert at the front is O(1); insert at the end, search and delete need a walk, O(n). CafeFlow keeps users, menu, orders and every cart in linked lists.
  • Stack (LIFO): push and pop at the top. CafeFlow uses it for the bill (bracket check and postfix), the cart Undo and the admin activity log.
  • Queue (FIFO): enqueue at the rear, dequeue at the front. Every counter has one queue of pending orders, and a counter can only accept the order at the front.
  • Pick a tab, press a button and watch each pointer move. The code panel highlights the exact line of ds.py that is running.
